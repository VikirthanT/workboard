# Work Board: how a manager uses it

A walkthrough script for stakeholders. About 3 minutes. **Say** is what to read aloud. **Show** is what is on screen.
Use the demo data (Manager 1 on the NPI team) so no real names appear.

**The one-line idea:** the manager gives work to their team, sees where every task stands, and keeps a record of
what happened. Only managers sign in. Team members are the people tasks are assigned to; they never log in.

---

## 1. Sign in and see your board (30 seconds)

**Show:** the sign-in page, then the board.

**Say:** "The manager signs in with their company Google account. The board shows only their own team's tasks. Every
task is a card, and the five columns are the stages of the work: **To Do, In Progress, Blocked, Review, Done**.
The colour on the card's edge is its priority."

## 2. Create and assign a task (35 seconds)

**Show:** click **Add task**, fill in the form, save.

**Say:** "To give someone work, press Add task. Add a title, choose who on your team it is for, set a priority and a
due date, and save. The card appears in To Do."

## 3. Move it along (30 seconds)

**Show:** drag a card from To Do to In Progress, then to Review.

**Say:** "Team members do not log in, so the manager moves the cards by dragging. Every move is recorded with who made
it and when."

## 4. When it is stuck, and when it is finished (40 seconds)

**Show:** drag a card to Blocked and type a reason; press **Resume**. Then drag a card to Done and write remarks.

**Say:** "If something is in the way, drag the card to Blocked. The system asks what is in the way and shows it on the
card. When it is sorted, press Resume. To close a task, drag it to Done and write remarks about what was checked.
A Done task is locked, so the record cannot be changed."

## 5. History and what needs attention (25 seconds)

**Show:** open a card and click the **Timeline** tab; then tick **Needs attention**.

**Say:** "Opening a card shows its full history: every move, reason and comment, in order. And Needs attention shows,
with a count, everything overdue, blocked or waiting in Review, so the manager knows where to look first."

## 6. Your team and the report (20 seconds)

**Show:** avatar menu, **My team**, then back on the board press **Report**.

**Say:** "From My team, the manager adds or removes people on their team. The Report button summarises how much is
done, what is open and what is late."

---

## Closing line (10 seconds)

**Say:** "Create a task, assign it, move it as work progresses, give a reason when it is blocked, close it with
remarks. Everything is recorded, and the board always shows what needs attention."

## Presenter notes

- Keep to the demo data. Do not show the real people directory.
- Slack notifications and an Accept step for team members are planned for a later phase. Today the manager moves
  every card.
- Admins can view any team's board read-only. That is outside this script, which is about the manager.
